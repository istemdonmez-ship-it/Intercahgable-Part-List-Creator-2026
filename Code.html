Critical Wearing Parts

High Consumption | 50-150% Stock
Parts with high wear rates and consumption. DIN 24296 recommends higher quantities due to frequent replacement needs.
Table 32: 2-4 sets for small fleets, 50-150% for 10+ pumps
Examples (from Table 32): • Sealing elements: 4-12 sets
• Gland packing: 4-8 sets
• Wear rings: 2-4 pieces
• Shaft sleeves: 2-4 pieces
🟡

Mechanical Components

Predictable Life | 20-30% Stock
Components with predictable MTBF. DIN 24296 Table 32 specifies 1-2 pieces for small fleets, scaling to percentages for larger installations.
Table 32: 1-2 pieces for small fleets, 20-30% for 10+ pumps
Examples (from Table 32): • Shafts: 1-2 pieces (20% for 10+)
• Impellers: 1-2 pieces (20% for 10+)
• Coupling elements: 1-4 pieces (30% for 10+)
🟢

Bearings & Rolling Elements

Scheduled Replacement | 25% Stock
All bearing types (radial ball, roller, angular contact). DIN 24296 specifies consistent quantities due to scheduled replacement intervals.
Table 32: 1-3 pieces, 25% for 10+ pumps
Examples (from Table 32): • Rolling element bearings: 1-3 sets
• Radial ball bearings: 1-3 pieces
• Radial roller bearings: 1-3 pieces

Enable this feature to apply the exact DIN 24296 Table 32 lookup for spare parts quantities. The system will automatically select the correct quantity based on:
  • Fleet Size: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6-7, 8-9, or 10+ pumps
  • Part Category: Automatic detection (shaft, impeller, bearing, seal, etc.)
  • Part Number Match: Identical material numbers are pooled

Example: For 12 pumps with identical mechanical seals:
  • ❌ Without optimization: 12 seals (1 per pump)
  • ✅ With DIN 24296 Table 32: 3 seals (25% × 12 = 3)
  • 💰 Savings: 9 seals (75% reduction)
