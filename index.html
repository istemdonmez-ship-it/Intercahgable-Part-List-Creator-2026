Recommended spare parts stock for 2 years' operation

This application implements the exact DIN 24296 Table 32 lookup table for centrifugal pump spare parts stock calculation. Quantities are based on fleet size (2-10+ pumps) and part criticality, not simple percentages.

📦 DIN 24296 Part Categories

Parts are automatically classified into these DIN 24296 standard categories:

  • Shafts — Rotating shafts
  • Impellers — Pump impellers and wheels
  • Bearings — Ball, roller, and sliding bearings
  • Seals — Mechanical seals, gaskets, O-rings
  • Wear Parts — Wear rings, sleeves, protection elements
  • Accessories — Couplings and other components
