⚙️ DIN 24296 Part Categories

Parts are automatically classified into three DIN 24296 categories based on their designation and part number. The system uses the official Table 32 lookup to recommend stock quantities.
🔴

Rotating Parts (Shafts, Impellers)

High Consumption | 50–150% Stock
Critical rotating components subject to wear and fatigue. High stock levels required due to long lead times and critical nature.
Table 32: 1 pc for 2–4 pumps | 2 pcs for 5–9 pumps | 20% for 10+
Examples: Pump shaft, impeller, shaft sleeve, balance disc
🟡

Bearings & Sealing Components

Predictable Life | 20–30% Stock
Components with predictable service life requiring scheduled replacement. Stock based on fleet size to ensure availability during maintenance windows.
Table 32: 1 pc for 2–3 pumps | 2–3 pcs for 4–9 pumps | 25% for 10+
Examples: Rolling bearings, ball bearings, mechanical seals, wear rings
🟢

Sealing Elements & Gaskets

Scheduled Replacement | 25% Stock
Consumable sealing components replaced at every overhaul. High stock quantities required as complete sets are needed for each maintenance.
Table 32: 4–6 sets for 2–3 pumps | 8–12 sets for 4–9 pumps | 150% for 10+
Examples: O-rings, gaskets, packing sets, gland packing, sealing kits

🔄 Interchangeable Parts Analysis

When enabled, parts found in multiple pumps will use DIN 24296 Table 32 quantities instead of multiplying by the number of pumps. This can significantly reduce your required stock when pumps share common parts.

For example: 3 pumps × 1 mechanical seal each = 3 standard | DIN 24296 = 1 optimized
